# Custom GPT Portfolio Brand Catalog

**Catalog date:** 2026-07-20  
**Evidence basis:** supplied URLs, public GPT landing cards, and the signed-in **My GPTs** dashboard. The account dashboard listed **89 GPTs**: 26 `Everyone`, 42 `Anyone with a link`, and 21 `Only me`. It supplied current visibility, short descriptions, and coarse chat-count bands. It does not expose instructions, knowledge files, enabled capabilities, version history, or detailed analytics. This is therefore a **portfolio-positioning catalog with light portfolio telemetry**, not a production-quality scorecard.

## Brand decision framework

| Brand | Owns | Test for fit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **OverKill Hill** | Promptcraft, AI architecture, AI-product systems, governed methods, reusable technical assets. | Would a practitioner use it to design, engineer, audit, or operationalize an AI system? |
| **Glee-fully** | Warm, practical, personalizable tools for everyday life, home, career, wellbeing, and discovery. | Does it help a person make life clearer, easier, more organized, or more enjoyable? |
| **AskJamie** | Jamie's professional perspective, judgement, and high-trust guidance for people and organizations. | Is the core value Jamie's counsel, diagnosis, or professional interpretation rather than a self-serve utility? |

`Primary` means the GPT should live under that brand. `Cross-brand` means it has a clear home but should link to or be powered by another brand. `Outside portfolio` means it is a client/vertical experiment or a third-party brand simulation, not a primary brand property.

## Portfolio findings

- **Glee-fully is the largest and clearest family.** Its personalizable-tools framing is coherent across career, home, food, travel, organization, wellbeing, and visual discovery.
- **OverKill Hill owns the specialist AI-production family.** The `-R` tools, PromptForge, and BrandGuard material are method and architecture products, not consumer utilities.
- **AskJamie has a small, credible professional-advisory nucleus.** Enterprise Sleuth, Professional Portfolio, and Resume Representative belong there. BrandGuard is better treated as an OverKill Hill product presented through AskJamie.
- **The corporate and archery GPTs should be separated from the three-brand catalog.** They may be prototypes, client concepts, or vertical experiments, but they should not dilute the public brand architecture without a named partnership or product strategy.
- **Visible attribution needs normalization.** The inspected Glee-fully and AskJamie GPT cards show `By overkillhill.com`, even when their title uses another brand. This is technically accurate only if OverKill Hill is intentionally the parent/publisher. If the brands are meant to stand independently, update Builder Profile, public profile/domain, and GPT descriptions together.

## Portfolio actions

1. Make Glee-fully Personalizable Tools the visible routing hub and group the Glee GPTs into career, home, food, travel, organization, wellbeing, and visual discovery.
2. Publish a small number of focused brand-entry GPTs rather than asking visitors to choose from a flat list. Keep specialist tools discoverable from each hub.
3. Resolve the public publisher line for every GPT. If `overkillhill.com` is the intentional parent publisher, say so consistently in all three brands' sites and descriptions. If not, publish under the relevant brand profile/domain.
4. Keep BrandGuard as an OverKill Hill artifact with an AskJamie expert-facing pathway. This protects the distinction between productized method and personal counsel.
5. Before promotion, inspect each Builder configuration and score it with the seven-dimension rubric in `.agents/skills/okhp3-custom-gpt-builder/references/quality-tiers.md`. No production tier can be inferred from the public card alone.
6. Run a safety and claims review before promoting the wellbeing, medication, home-estimate, and third-party-brand GPTs.
