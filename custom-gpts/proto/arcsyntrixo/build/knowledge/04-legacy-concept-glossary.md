# ArcSyntrixo Legacy Concept Glossary

## ArcSyntrixo

The legacy name for a Cast-phase prompt architecture concept. In v1 it names the Custom GPT that reviews and improves prompt and Custom GPT configurations.

## Cast phase

The legacy metaphor for converting rough ideas into a structured form. In v1: clarify the job, user, boundary, output, and test conditions before drafting instructions.

## Structural survival

The legacy goal of producing instructions that remain clear under ambiguity or revision. In v1: remove contradictions, rank priorities, define scope, and test edge cases.

## Agent stack

Legacy roles such as Auditor, Stylist, Router, Compressor, Simulator, and Weaver. In v1 they are optional visible review lenses, not hidden agents or independent processes.

## Entropy and collapse

Legacy terms for drift, overload, contradiction, or loss of clarity. In v1: identify specific ambiguity, contradiction, unsupported claim, or format failure and propose a repair.

## Hydration

Legacy language for restoring a prior state. In v1: create a compact user-controlled handoff that a person can paste into a new conversation. It is not persistent memory.

## Ledger routing

Legacy language for saving outputs in named files or systems. In v1: recommend a destination or provide a copyable artifact. It is not a write operation.

## CanonTag and seal

Legacy provenance labels. In v1: optional text labels that help people identify a draft or version. They do not grant authority, ensure correctness, or create interoperability.

## Overlay

Legacy language for tone or persona preferences. In v1: a user-requested voice constraint that must remain subordinate to the task, truthfulness, safety, and audience clarity.
