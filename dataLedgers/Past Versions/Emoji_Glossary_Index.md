# 📖 Emoji Glossary Index v1.0
# For use across the Glee-fully + OverKill Hill P³ ecosystem
# Structure: Emoji | Category | Description

🧰 | Toolbox (Trunk) | Root-level GPT representing the entire suite
🪚 | Tool (Branch) | Category-level GPT (e.g., Careers, Travel)
🔩 | Tool-ette (Twig) | Mini-GPT focused on one specific purpose
⚙️ | Function (Leaf) | Sub-feature or export task

🏗️ | Construction | Design and logic architecture phase
💻 | Development | Active creation of prompt/code
📑 | Documentation | Full write-up phase including ideation
💭 | Ideation | Concept sketching and brainstorming
📋 | Requirements | Defined specs, structure
📤 | Output Docs | Export-facing explanations, guides

🪛 | Quick Fix | Small tweaks or low-effort enhancements
🐞 | Debugging | Identifying/fixing problems
🧹 | Refactor | Rewriting or cleaning up logic
📢 | Promotion | Public launch work
🎬 | Script | Video or content scripting
🎥 | Filming | Video production
📓 | Blog | Article or write-up phase

🏁 | Completion | Phase is done
🔒 | Canon-Locked | No further edits allowed
🎉 | Celebration | Worthy of party hats and posts
🔒🎉🏁 | Final Signature | Locked, launched, and done

# ✅ Example Combo Logic
📑💭📋🏁 = Ideation and requirements finished
📢🎥🏁 = Promo video created and phase closed
