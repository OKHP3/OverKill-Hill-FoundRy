When self publishing through Amazon how much do you keep 
====#[REPLY]#
When self-publishing through Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP), your royalty rate and earnings depend on the format, pricing, and distribution options you choose. Here's a clear breakdown:

1. eBooks (Kindle Format)
Royalty Options:

35% Royalty: You earn 35% of the list price (minus delivery costs, which are usually negligible).

70% Royalty: You earn 70% of the list price if:

You price the book between $2.99 and $9.99.

The customer is in a supported territory (e.g., US, UK, Canada, etc.).

You allow Amazon to distribute the eBook in all territories where you hold rights.

Delivery fee (usually $0.10–$0.15/MB) is deducted from the 70% rate.

✅ Example: A $4.99 eBook sold at 70% earns you around $3.45 after Amazon's cut and delivery costs.

2. Paperback (Print-on-Demand)
Royalty Rate: 60% of the list price, minus printing costs.

✅ Example:

List price: $14.99

Amazon cut: 40% → you get 60% = $8.99

Printing cost (say $4.50): $8.99 - $4.50 = $4.49 royalty

Color interiors, page count, and paper type significantly affect the printing cost.

3. Hardcover (if enabled)
Same royalty model as paperbacks (60% - printing cost), but print costs are higher, so royalties are typically lower unless priced higher.

Other Notes:
There are no upfront fees to publish.

Amazon sets minimum and maximum pricing based on printing and delivery requirements.

You can distribute to other markets (Expanded Distribution), but royalty drops to 40% for paperbacks sold outside Amazon.
